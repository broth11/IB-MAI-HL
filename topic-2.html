Syllabus content
  • Concept of a function, domain, range and graph.
  • Function notation, for example $f(x), v(t), C(n)$.
  • The concept of a function as a mathematical model.
  • GuidanceExample: $f(x)=\sqrt{2-x}$, the domain is $x \leq 2$, range is $f(x) \geq 0$.A graph is helpful in visualizing the range.
  • Informal concept that an inverse function reverses or undoes the effect of a function.
  • GuidanceExample: Solving $f(x)=10$ is equivalent to finding $f^{-1}(10)$.
  • Inverse function as a reflection in the line $y=x$, and the notation $f^{-1}(x)$.
  • GuidanceStudents should be aware that inverse functions exist for one to one functions; the domain of $f^{-1}(x)$ is equal to the range of $f(x)$.

AHLStudy guide
Files
  • Nothing yet
Videos

Syllabus content
  • Transformations of graphs.
  • GuidanceStudents will be expected to be able to perform transformations on all functions from the SL and AHL section of this topic, and others in the context of modelling real-life situations.
  • Translations: $y=f(x)+b ; y=f(x-a)$.
  • Reflections: in the $x$ axis $y=-f(x)$, and in the $y$ axis $y=f(-x)$.
  • GuidanceTranslation by the vector $\binom{3}{-2}$ denotes horizontal translation of 3 units to the right, and vertical translation of 2 units down.
  • Vertical stretch with scale factor $p: y=p f(x)$.
  • Horizontal stretch with scale factor $\frac{1}{q}: y=f(q x)$
  • Guidance$x$ and $y$ axes are invariant.
  • Composite transformations.
  • GuidanceStudents should be made aware of the significance of the order of transformations.Example: $y=x^{2}$ used to obtain $y=3 x^{2}+2$ by a vertical stretch of scale factor 3 followed by a translation of $\binom{0}{2}$.Example: $y=\sin x$ used to obtain $y=4 \sin 2 x$ by a vertical stretch of scale factor 4 and a horizontal stretch of scale factor $\frac{1}{2}$.
